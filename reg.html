01 / PRIVACY & ELIGIBILITY

PERSONAL SPACE COMES FIRST
01

MUTUAL BOUNDARIES

Privacy

I deeply respect the ethics of personal space and mutual boundaries. Please refrain from prying too far into my life beyond the virtual persona of Rado Deadlo Garvios, especially matters concerning my private life.

Casual conversations about daily life are more than welcome, as long as they do not delve into personal identity. Sharing voice messages or recording our calls is strictly prohibited.

02

HONESTY & RESPECT

Eligibility

You must be at least 20 years old for a romantic engagement or 19 years old for a platonic one. Please be honest about your age—manipulating it is not acceptable.

Respect is the key to any meeting, both in words and actions.

02 / AVAILABILITY

TIME, PLATFORMS & ROLES
03

DAILY CAPACITY

When & how

I only accommodate two clients per day; the highest-tier package is exclusively for you alone. I am available from 10 AM to 10 PM, though flexible-time packages are exempt from this schedule.

We can meet as lovers, friends, siblings, or even parental figures—whatever role brings you comfort.

04

VIRTUAL ACCOUNTS ONLY

Platforms

You can find me on Twitter/X, Telegram, or Discord. As this is strictly a virtual engagement, I will only accept accounts that fall under CA/BA/RP/FA/Rants. Accounts containing real-life or personal information will not be accepted.

I offer voice messages and call services, but please discuss calls with me first to ensure I am in the right condition to engage.

03 / RESPONSE & SESSION TIME

PLEASE ALLOW ME SOME TIME
05

RESPONSE TIME

When I reply

I will reply within 5 to 60 minutes. On weekdays between 10 AM and 3 PM, I may be slower to respond due to work commitments—please be patient with me.

You’re welcome to send multiple messages while I’m occupied; I don’t mind. Just keep in mind that I’m human too, with moments for meals, showers, and rest.

06

DURING OUR TIME TOGETHER

Session length

Our meetings can last anywhere from a minimum of 1 hour to a maximum of 7 days per session. Any following meeting must be scheduled at least 7 days after our last encounter.

Unless specifically requested otherwise, I will continue posting promotional content during our time together. A meeting may be discontinued if either party violates any policies.

04 / PAYMENT & AFTERCARE

BOOKING, FEEDBACK & EXPECTATIONS
07

PAYMENT & SCHEDULING

Booking

To book a session, please check my availability with my manager. If our schedules align, you’ll need to fill out a form carefully to avoid any miscommunication.

Payment is required upfront to prevent hit-and-run cases. Refunds are not available unless I am at fault, but rescheduling is possible in case of unexpected circumstances.

08

AFTER OUR TIME TOGETHER

Feedback

You are required to fill out a Padlet review before scheduling another meeting. A new session cannot be arranged until the previous feedback has been completed.

Any feelings that arise beyond our sessions will be respected, but please remember—I am merely providing a service, and I cannot hold responsibility for emotions beyond that scope.
